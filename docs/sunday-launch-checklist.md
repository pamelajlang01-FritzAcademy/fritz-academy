# Fritz Academy — Sunday Launch Acceptance Checklist

Goal: teacher can start and complete a real lesson Sunday morning without improvising around broken software.

## Must pass before Sunday

### Access and launch
- Academy opens from production URL on desktop and mobile/tablet.
- Student can enter the intended lesson without admin-only controls.
- Teacher can reach lesson controls/teacher view where applicable.
- No dead links, 404s, missing required assets, console-breaking errors, or blocked media.

### Lesson 1 end-to-end
- Greeting loads and student response works.
- Alphabet/song media plays with sound.
- A/B phonics interaction works.
- Vocabulary interaction responds correctly.
- Illustrated story loads in correct order.
- Comprehension interaction accepts and evaluates response.
- Reader opens and is legible.
- Builder reward is earned exactly once.
- Reward can be placed/used in builder area.
- Progress saves after refresh/re-entry.
- Completion unlocks the intended next lesson and nothing unintended.

### Opening sequence
- Repeat the same checks for all Sunday-available opening lessons.
- No new lesson may require a one-off game-engine mechanic.
- If a later lesson is incomplete, it remains locked/hidden rather than exposing broken content.

### Media quality
- Character visuals match established Fritz dog characters.
- No human characters appear in Fritz story/game episodes.
- Images are not stretched, malformed, blurry, watermarked, or visibly placeholder quality.
- Audio is understandable, age-appropriate, and at a consistent volume.
- Videos play without requiring a paid external account during class.

### Instructional quality
- Activity changes every 2–4 minutes.
- Total lesson target is about 20–25 minutes.
- English targets are explicit in teacher notes but embedded naturally in game/story for student.
- Phonics sequence matches lesson target.
- Reader difficulty matches the taught language.
- Student receives enough repetition without repetitive screen design.
- No harmful, frightening, humiliating, deceptive, or developmentally inappropriate content.

### Placement/progress
- Shared RAE English scale is available to teacher.
- Entry assessment result can identify an appropriate starting point.
- Completion count alone does not assign proficiency.

### Commercial readiness minimum
- Product name, description, price and lesson frequency are consistent wherever displayed.
- Parent-facing explanation clearly states the 108-lesson beginner pathway.
- Support/contact path works.
- Privacy/terms/payment wording is present wherever the product is being sold.
- No test-mode payment or admin bypass is exposed to customers.

## Sunday fallback rule
If a nonessential feature fails QA, disable/hide it and preserve the complete teaching path. Never destabilize the opening lesson to finish a later feature.
