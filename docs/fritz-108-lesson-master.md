# Fritz Academy — 108-Lesson Curriculum Master

Status: content master for 36 weeks × 3 lessons. This document defines the teaching/content layer only. Existing game mechanics are reused; new episodes must not require one-off engine changes.

Core rules:
- 3 lessons/week, about 25 minutes each.
- Episodic dog-world story first; instruction is embedded in play.
- Established Fritz dog characters only. No human characters.
- Songs are recurring Music Box rewards and are reused across many lessons.
- Every lesson includes: story beat, language target, short phonics/reading work, interactive game task, reader/key-story reinforcement, and builder reward.
- Same RAE English scale is used for placement/progress, cross-walked to CEFR.
- No harmful, frightening, humiliating, deceptive, or developmentally inappropriate content.
- Activity mode changes every 2–4 minutes.

## Music Box anchor library
1. Alphabet Song — reused through completion of the alphabet and later review.
2. Numbers Song — counting, quantities, simple number language.
3. Colors Song — colors and simple descriptions.
4. Feelings Song — happy, sad, tired, excited, scared, angry, calm, proud, worried.
5. Family Song — immediate and extended family, older/younger.
6. Animals Song — common animals, actions, habitats, sounds where useful.
7. Professions Song — community jobs and what people/dogs in role-play do; represented through the dog world rather than human characters.
8. Actions Song — run, walk, jump, sit, stand, turn, clap, point, open, close, give, show.
9. Body Song — body parts and movement.
10. Food Song — common foods, likes/dislikes, simple meal language.
11. Daily Routine Song — wake up, wash, dress, eat, go, play, sleep.
12. Weather Song — sunny, rainy, windy, cloudy, hot, cold.
13. Places Song — school, home, park, shop, library, field, clinic, station, etc. as dog-world locations.

The final song list may be reduced if one anchor song can carry multiple topics without weakening repetition.

## Weeks 5–8 — Me, Feelings, Actions, Body

### Week 5 — How I Feel
**5-A** The Missing Morning Smile — happy/sad/tired/excited; “I feel ___.” Music Box: Feelings Song unlock. Builder: emotion flags.  
**5-B** Rascal’s Big Surprise — surprised/worried/calm/proud; “Are you ___?” “Yes, I am/No, I’m not.” Builder: expression badges.  
**5-C** Feelings Trail — choose the right feeling from story clues; short reader “Fritz Feels Fine.” Builder: feelings bench/sign.

### Week 6 — Move With Fritz
**6-A** Follow Fritz — stand/sit/turn/clap/point. Music Box: Actions Song. Builder: movement markers.  
**6-B** Bash and Bear’s Direction Race — walk/run/jump/stop/go; older/younger used only if relationship is already introduced visually. Builder: path pieces.  
**6-C** Two-Step Mission — follow two-step directions; reader “Go, Stop, Turn.” Builder: challenge gate.

### Week 7 — My Body
**7-A** Head to Paw — head/eyes/ears/nose/mouth/legs/feet. Music Box: Body Song. Builder: mirror station.  
**7-B** Nola’s Stretch Break — arms/legs/back/turn/bend/stretch; “Touch your ___.” Builder: exercise mat.  
**7-C** Body Clue Game — identify body parts from clues and actions; reader “I Can Move.” Builder: wellness corner.

### Week 8 — What I Can Do
**8-A** Fritz Tries Something New — can/cannot + actions.  
**8-B** Practice Makes Progress — “I can ___ now.” simple before/after language.  
**8-C** Academy Skill Show — cumulative feelings/actions/body speaking mission. Builder: skill-show stage.

## Weeks 33–36 — Connected Speech and Beginner Graduation

### Week 33 — Questions and Answers
**33-A** Who? — person/character identity questions in dog-world cast.  
**33-B** What and Where? — familiar object/place questions.  
**33-C** When? — morning/afternoon/night and routine questions. Builder: question board.

### Week 34 — Connect Your Ideas
**34-A** And — connect two simple ideas.  
**34-B** But — simple contrast: “I like apples, but I don’t like…”  
**34-C** Then — sequence two or three actions. Builder: story path.

### Week 35 — Tell a Short Story
**35-A** Beginning — who/where.  
**35-B** Middle — what happened/then.  
**35-C** Ending — result/feeling. Learner retells a very short dog-world event in connected simple sentences. Builder: story stage.

### Week 36 — First-Year Academy Mission
**36-A** Listening Mission — multi-step familiar directions, short listening comprehension.  
**36-B** Reading and Speaking Mission — short reader + connected answers.  
**36-C** Builder Graduation Adventure — cumulative game episode covering greetings, feelings, family, numbers, colors, animals, food, routines, weather, places, professions, and connected simple sentences. Builder: graduation district + Music Box celebration.

## Progress checkpoints
- **Week 4:** Alphabet Foundation Mission — diagnostic, game-like, not a formal test.
- **Week 8:** Me & Actions checkpoint.
- **Week 12:** Family & connected-sentence checkpoint.
- **Week 18:** vocabulary/listening/reader checkpoint.
- **Week 24:** routine sequencing and short connected speech.
- **Week 30:** community-language checkpoint.
- **Week 36:** full beginner-course progress assessment using the shared RAE/CEFR-aligned scale.

Completion of a lesson count never automatically assigns a proficiency level. Placement/progress is based on demonstrated listening, speaking, reading and writing/early-writing skills appropriate to age.

## Sunday-readiness rule
For Sunday teaching, Weeks 1–4 receive deepest runtime QA first. Later lesson content can be loaded in batches after the opening sequence is verified end-to-end. The 108-lesson master is the content source of truth; it does not justify destabilizing working game functionality.
