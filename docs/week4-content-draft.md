# Fritz Academy — Week 4 Content Draft

Purpose: finish the first alphabet arc with W, X, Y, Z while reviewing previously learned English through builder play.

## 4-A — Weather Watch — W/X

### Language goals
- Ask and answer: What is the weather like?
- Use: It is sunny / rainy / windy / cloudy.
- Review colors and simple counting in weather scenes.

### Phonics
- W/w: water, wind, web
- X/x: box, fox, six (focus on hearing /ks/ in familiar words rather than forcing only initial-X words)

### Short-burst lesson flow
1. Fritz weather greeting: choose today's weather.
2. Reuse alphabet song; spotlight W and X.
3. W/X sound taps.
4. Weather picture-choice game.
5. Story: "The Windy Welcome Sign" — the friends repair a sign after a windy morning.
6. Three-question story check.
7. Reader: "Wind and Water".
8. Builder task: place 2 blue rain barrels beside the garden and 3 yellow wind flags near the gate.
9. Reward reveal.

### Builder rewards
- Weather flag
- Blue rain barrel
- W/X letter sign
- Wind spinner
- Weather station

## 4-B — Yes, You Can! — Y/Z

### Language goals
- Use yes / no answers.
- Use: I can ___. / I cannot ___.
- Use simple action verbs already encountered plus jump, zip, zigzag.

### Phonics
- Y/y: yellow, yes, yarn
- Z/z: zebra, zero, zip

### Short-burst lesson flow
1. Fritz asks: Can you clap? Can you stand? Can you jump?
2. Alphabet song; spotlight Y and Z.
3. Y/Z phonics micro-game.
4. Yes/No movement game.
5. Story: "Zigzag to the Yellow Gate".
6. Story check.
7. Reader: "Yes, I Can!"
8. Builder task using colors, numbers, and actions.
9. Reward reveal.

### Builder rewards
- Yellow gate
- Zigzag path
- Y/Z banner
- Action wheel
- Zebra garden marker

## 4-C — Alphabet Builder Challenge — A-Z Review

### Language goals
- Review letter names and common sounds.
- Review color, number, shape, classroom, position, and action vocabulary.
- Practice simple complete sentences instead of isolated recall.

### Short-burst lesson flow
1. Fritz alphabet warm-up.
2. Reusable alphabet song.
3. Four rapid letter hunts: A-F, G-N, O-V, W-Z.
4. Match beginning sounds to familiar pictures.
5. Story: "The Missing Alphabet Tiles".
6. Reader: "Twenty-Six Letters".
7. Mixed-language builder challenge: e.g. place three red circles under the W sign; put one blue square beside the B sign.
8. A-Z celebration reward.

### Builder rewards
- Alphabet arch
- A-Z stepping stones
- Letter lanterns
- Alphabet tree
- Academy alphabet plaque

## 4-D — My First Academy Mission — Cumulative Review

### Language goals
- Introduce yourself.
- Say how you feel.
- Identify colors, numbers, shapes, classroom objects, and positions.
- Follow two-step directions.
- Read short familiar sentences.
- Demonstrate A-Z recognition at an introductory level.

### Mission structure
This lesson should feel like a game mission rather than a test. Captain Fritz sends the student through several short stations.

1. Greeting Gate — name and feeling.
2. Letter Bridge — quick A-Z recognition.
3. Color Garden — identify and place colored objects.
4. Counting Path — count 1-10.
5. Shape Station — identify shapes.
6. Learning Room — follow two-step classroom directions.
7. Story Stop — short illustrated reading and comprehension.
8. Builder Finale — use earned pieces to complete the Week 4 scene.
9. Academy celebration.

### Final Week 4 rewards
- Mission flag
- Builder badge
- Alphabet trophy
- Celebration lights
- Completed First Academy District / scene

## Media reuse plan

Do not require a unique song for each Week 4 lesson. Reuse:
- Alphabet song in 4-A, 4-B, and 4-C.
- Welcome/Academy theme as lesson close where it still fits.
- New short W/X and Y/Z phonics clips only if visual/audio quality materially improves learning.
- A single alphabet celebration clip can serve both 4-C and 4-D.

## Attention-span rule

No passive segment should dominate the lesson. Each lesson should switch mode every 2-4 minutes among listening, tapping, speaking, story, movement, reading, choosing, and building.
