FRITZ ACADEMY — HOW ARE YOU TODAY?
Target length: 65–80 seconds
Style: warm upbeat pop with a confident classroom-game feel; clear diction; not babyish; gentle build into a memorable chorus.
Asset filename after ElevenLabs render: assets/how-are-you-today-song.mp3

[INTRO]
Captain Fritz says, “Welcome back!
Before our adventure starts,
tell us how you feel today.”

[VERSE 1]
Maybe I am happy.
Maybe I am tired.
Maybe I am nervous,
or excited and inspired.

Maybe I feel calm today.
Maybe I feel sad.
Maybe I feel proud of something,
or surprised by what I had.

[CHORUS]
How are you today?
Tell me how you feel.
Choose the words that fit you best.
Your feelings all are real.
How are you today?
Say it loud and clear.
I am happy. I am tired.
I am glad that I am here.

[VERSE 2]
Feelings can be different
from morning until night.
We can name them, talk about them,
and find the words that fit just right.

[FINAL CHORUS]
How are you today?
Tell me how you feel.
Use your words and share your thought.
Tell us what is real.
How are you today?
Our adventure starts right here.
At Fritz Academy we listen,
and we’re glad that you are here.

Production note: Keep emotion words crisp and easy to imitate. Avoid exaggerated preschool vocals. This should work as a recurring lesson-opening song for beginner ESL students.
