FRITZ ACADEMY — QUESTION WORDS SONG
Target length: 70–90 seconds
Style: bright adventure-pop, catchy but not babyish; clear diction; moderate tempo; energetic percussion; friendly ensemble chorus.
Asset filename after ElevenLabs render: assets/question-words-song.mp3

[INTRO]
Captain Fritz has a question.
The Academy needs a clue.
Ask the right question word,
and find out what is true!

[VERSE 1]
WHO tells us the person.
WHAT tells us the thing.
WHERE tells us the place we go.
WHEN tells us the time.

[CHORUS]
Who? What? Where? When?
Ask it, think, then ask again.
Why? How? Follow the clue.
Good questions help us find what is true!

[VERSE 2]
WHY asks for a reason.
HOW asks for the way.
Captain Fritz says, “Think it through.
What should we ask today?”

[CALL AND RESPONSE]
Who? — The person!
What? — The thing!
Where? — The place!
When? — The time!
Why? — The reason!
How? — The way!

[FINAL CHORUS]
Who? What? Where? When?
Ask it, think, then ask again.
Why? How? Follow the clue.
At Fritz Academy, questions help us learn something new!

Production note: Prioritize intelligibility of WHO / WHAT / WHERE / WHEN / WHY / HOW. Do not over-sing the vocabulary. No childish nursery-rhyme voice.
